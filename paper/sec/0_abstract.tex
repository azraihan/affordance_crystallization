\begin{abstract}
Self-supervised video models are strong backbones for robotic perception.
V-JEPA~2.1 extends V-JEPA~2 by applying its predictive objective hierarchically at
intermediate encoder layers, yet no prior work examines whether this reorganizes
\emph{where} affordance information crystallizes inside the network.
We probe 25 layer positions of architecturally matched ViT-L encoders
(V-JEPA~2, V-JEPA~2.1, DINOv2) on the UMD Part Affordance Dataset and show that
V-JEPA~2.1's deep supervision shifts the peak affordance layer earlier,
concentrates signal into a sparser set of attention heads,
and enables a $4{\times}$ attention compute reduction with minimal mAP loss.
\end{abstract}
