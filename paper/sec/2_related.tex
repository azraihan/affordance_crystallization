\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Video self-supervised learning.}
V-JEPA~2~\cite{bardes2025vjepa2} trains a video encoder by predicting masked spatio-temporal
regions in a learned latent space.
V-JEPA~2.1~\cite{bardes2026vjepa21} extends this with a Dense Predictive Loss applied
hierarchically at intermediate layers, leading to a 20-point improvement in robot grasping.
DINOv2~\cite{oquab2024dinov2} provides a strong static-image self-supervised baseline.

\paragraph{Mechanistic interpretability.}
Elhage et al.~\cite{elhage2021circuits} establish a mathematical framework for understanding
which computational structures within transformers carry specific information.
Michel et al.~\cite{michel2019sixteen} show that most attention heads in BERT can be pruned
without accuracy loss, identifying a small set of important heads.
Alain and Bengio~\cite{alain2016probes} introduce linear classifier probes for localizing
information across network layers.

\paragraph{Affordance detection.}
Myers et al.~\cite{myers2015affordance} introduce the UMD Part Affordance Dataset.
No prior work examines which transformer layers or heads carry affordance information
in frozen video encoders.
