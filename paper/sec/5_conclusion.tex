\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

We have shown mechanistically that V-JEPA~2.1's intermediate-layer supervision
reshapes where affordance information lives: features peak earlier and concentrate
in a sparser set of attention heads compared to V-JEPA~2.
Two practical implications emerge for robotic perception:
(1) encoders can terminate at the peak layer rather than running full depth;
(2) attention-head sparsification at the peak layer offers a free efficiency gain
with minimal accuracy loss.
Future work should extend this analysis to task-conditioned settings and examine
whether the same heads generalize across affordance datasets and embodiments.
